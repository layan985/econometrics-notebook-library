# Advanced Extension Problems

These are not worksheet exercises. Each is intended to become a mergeable notebook/PR.

1. **Event-study estimator tournament:** compare TWFE, Sun–Abraham, Callaway–Sant'Anna, BJS imputation, stacked DiD, and two-stage DiD under heterogeneity, anticipation, and changing controls; report bias, RMSE, coverage, and horizon support.
2. **Few-treated-cluster stress test:** vary treated clusters while comparing CR1, small-sample corrections, Rademacher/Webb wild bootstrap, and design-based placebo inference.
3. **Conley cutoff falsification:** vary the true spatial-correlation range and estimator cutoff; separately simulate treatment spillovers to show that correct SEs cannot repair violated identification.
4. **Shift-share leave-one-out reconstruction:** induce own-observation bias in aggregate shocks, construct leave-one-out shocks, and add exposure/influential-shock diagnostics.
5. **Synthetic-control convex-hull failure:** move the treated unit outside donor support and compare classical, augmented/ridge, and matrix-completion approaches.
6. **Randomization inference under the wrong assignment mechanism:** simulate cluster randomization and quantify size distortion from individual-level permutations.
7. **LP versus VAR under misspecification:** compare responses in nonlinear/state-dependent systems and add simultaneous confidence bands.
